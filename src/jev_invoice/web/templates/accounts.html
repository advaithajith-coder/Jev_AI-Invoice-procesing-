Writing good descriptions

  • Name the things that belong here, the way invoices describe them: "toner, paper, pens".
  • Say what does not, when two accounts are close: "equipment under $2,500; above that, Computer Equipment".
  • Deactivate an account you no longer use instead of deleting it: past invoices still refer to it.
