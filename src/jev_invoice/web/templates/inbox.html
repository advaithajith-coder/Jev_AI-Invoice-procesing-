The route every invoice takes

  1. Scans and photos are transcribed by a vision model
  2. A language model reads the fields and lines
  3. Code checks the sums, dates, vendor, bank account and PO
  4. Jev judges fraud, payability and risk with probabilities
  5. A fixed policy approves it, or sends it to a person with reasons
