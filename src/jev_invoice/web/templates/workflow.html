Purchase to payable workflow

Start with the order

  1. 1
    Create PO

    Choose the vendor and add the items, quantities and prices. Tick Needs receiving for goods.

  2. 2
    Add delivery details

    Open the PO in Delivery Management and record the quantities that arrived. Report any missing or damaged goods.

    Service orders skip this step.
  3. 3
    Upload Invoice

    Upload the supplier invoice with its PO reference. The checks above compare it with the order and, for goods, the recorded deliveries.

Invoices can also arrive before delivery; goods invoices with quantities still outstanding need review until the delivery is recorded.
